What the forms collect

When you send a scenario through a form on this site, we receive what you type: your name, phone number, email address, the property area or address, how the property will be used, and any notes you add. The form also records the page you sent it from, the first page you landed on, and advertising campaign details from the link that brought you here (such as UTM tags and Google click IDs), so we can tell which advertising led to the inquiry. These campaign details are saved in your browser local storage so they can be attached when you send a form.

How we use it

We use this information to contact you by phone or email about your mortgage inquiry and to review your scenario. Sending a form is not a loan application and is not a credit decision, approval or commitment to lend. We also use the campaign details to measure how our advertising performs.

Analytics and advertising tools

  • Google Ads tag. Loads from Google and measures visits that come from Google advertising. When your request is confirmed on the thank-you page, it records a conversion for the advertising campaign.
  • Microsoft Clarity. Records how visitors use pages, including clicks, taps and scrolling, as session recordings and heatmaps. It may set cookies.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics. Provides aggregate measurements of page views and performance.

You can manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your requests

If you live in a state that gives you privacy rights, you may ask what information we hold about you or ask us to delete it. Call (310) 654-1577 and tell us which request you are making.
