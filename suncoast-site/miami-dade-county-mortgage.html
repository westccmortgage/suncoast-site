Miami-Dade FAQ

Questions worth answering early.

Can every Miami-Dade condo use conventional financing?

No. Project eligibility can depend on factors beyond the borrower's qualifications. The building and lender requirements need their own review.

Can a self-employed buyer use bank statements instead of tax returns?

Some Non-QM lenders offer bank-statement programs, but documentation, deposits, business ownership, reserves and other requirements vary. A complete scenario review is required.

Do you review investment properties?

Yes. Depending on the file, conventional investor financing, DSCR or other Non-QM paths may be considered.

I live outside the United States. Where should I start?

Use the dedicated Florida Sun Mortgage international-buyer review so country, status, U.S. credit history, income documentation and property use are captured correctly.
