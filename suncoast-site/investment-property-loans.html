Florida Investment Property Loans: Conventional vs. DSCR vs. Non-QM

Direct answer: A Florida rental property does not automatically require DSCR or Non-QM. Some investors may have a conventional Agency path; DSCR or another Non-QM structure may fit other scenarios. The comparison depends on borrower profile, property type, rental history, documentation and current guidelines.

Conventional

Agency rental-income rules may work when borrower, property and documentation requirements are satisfied.

DSCR

Investment-property financing focused primarily on property cash flow rather than traditional employment income.

Non-QM

Alternative documentation or property structures may be available when an Agency execution is not the best fit.

What about short-term rentals and Florida condos?

Direct answer: Both require scenario-specific review. Short-term-rental income treatment differs by program, and condo project eligibility, insurance, reserves, structural information and lender overlays may affect financing independently of the borrower.
