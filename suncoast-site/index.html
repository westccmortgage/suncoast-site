Condo first-look

For Florida condos, the property can be as important as the borrower.

A strong borrower does not automatically make every condominium project financeable. Project review, master insurance, reserves, litigation, structural or inspection-related information and lender overlays may affect available financing.

  • Send the property before or during offer strategy.
  • Identify whether a standard agency path appears realistic.
  • If not, organize alternatives without representing that any program is guaranteed.
