Broward FAQ

Questions to resolve before underwriting gets expensive.

Can a Broward condo fail financing even if the buyer is strong?

Yes. Condo project eligibility is separate from borrower qualification and can be affected by lender project requirements and building documentation.

Can rental income support an investment-property loan?

Potentially. The treatment of rent depends on the loan program and documentation. DSCR lenders use their own calculations and overlays.

Do you handle self-employed buyers?

Yes. We can review conventional documentation and, when appropriate, alternative-documentation programs such as bank-statement loans.

What if the buyer is a foreign national?

We route that scenario to Florida Sun Mortgage so foreign income, assets, U.S. credit history and residency/status details are captured correctly.
