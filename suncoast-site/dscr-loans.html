Florida DSCR Loans: Compare the Property-Cash-Flow Path

DSCR can be useful for investors, but it should not be assumed to be the only rental-property financing option.

Does Every Florida Rental Property Need a DSCR Loan?

Direct answer: No. Some rental-property borrowers may have a conventional Agency option, while others may be better served by DSCR or another Non-QM structure. We compare the borrower, property, rental-income documentation and current program rules rather than assuming one loan type.

Conventional

Potential fit when Agency borrower, property and rental-income requirements can be met.

DSCR

Potential fit when investment-property cash flow is the primary qualification path.

Other Non-QM

Potential fit for alternative documentation or scenarios outside standard Agency guidelines.

Can a short-term rental use conventional financing?

Direct answer: In some eligible scenarios, short-term-rental income may be considered under current Agency guidance. Legal STR use, property eligibility, rental history, appraisal or rent documentation and lender requirements matter. This is scenario analysis, not a qualification promise.
