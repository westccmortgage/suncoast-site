Palm Beach FAQ

Questions that change the lender strategy.

Is every large Palm Beach County mortgage automatically jumbo?

No. Loan classification depends on current loan limits, property location, loan amount and program. We review the actual structure rather than assume.

Can a business owner qualify using bank statements?

Some lenders offer bank-statement programs. The analysis can depend on business ownership, deposits, expense factors, reserves, credit and the rest of the file.

Can a second home be rented?

Occupancy and rental expectations must match program requirements. A property intended primarily as a rental may need investment-property treatment instead.

Do you handle international buyers in Boca Raton or Palm Beach?

Yes. Those files are routed to Florida Sun Mortgage's dedicated international-buyer review.
