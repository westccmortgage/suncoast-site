Which Mortgage Qualification Method Can Work for a Self-Employed Florida Borrower?

Direct answer: Self-employed does not automatically mean Non-QM. Compare traditional income documentation, bank-statement qualification, asset-based or asset-utilization approaches, and DSCR for an eligible investment property before selecting a structure.

Traditional Income Documentation

Agency or traditional underwriting may work when tax returns and applicable income analysis support the loan.

Bank Statement

Some Non-QM programs analyze eligible deposits and business-expense factors rather than taxable net income alone.

Asset-Based / Asset Utilization

Some programs can evaluate eligible assets using program-specific calculations and reserve rules.

DSCR for Investment Property

For eligible investment properties, DSCR may focus primarily on property cash flow rather than employment income.
